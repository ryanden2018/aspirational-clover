Biography

From an early age, I have been deeply interested in computers and the practice of software development. My earliest exposures to "real" programming, in high school, were in Java (especially Swing). Over the years I have worked on professional software project utilizing various technologies: Python, JavaScript, TypeScript, Java, and C#. My specialization is web development, especially in the context of bespoke business applications. Over the course of my six years of professional experience I have developed or maintained numerous applications, primarily in React but utilizing other technologies as well.

My educational background is in mathematics. I hold a Bachelor of Science degree in Applied and Computational Mathematics from California Institute of Technology, and a Doctor of Philosophy degree in Mathematics from New York University. Subsequently, I completed the R. H. Bing Fellowship program at The University of Texas at Austin. I also hold a Master of Engineering degree in Software Engineering from The University of Texas at Austin.
